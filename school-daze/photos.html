How to send a good photo

  • Send the original file, the biggest copy you have.
  • Wide (landscape) photos fill the screen best.
  • No screenshots. Avoid copies saved from Instagram, Facebook or a text message, which are shrunk.
More tips, and how to send a printed photo
  • A printed photo? Lay it flat near a window, with no flash. Hold your phone straight above it and fill the frame. Or scan it at 600 dpi. Google's free PhotoScan app removes glare.
  • JPEG, PNG and iPhone photos all work.
  • This page checks each photo's size and tells you how it will look.
  • You can send up to 5 photos. Large files are fine: this page shrinks each one to 6 MB or less before it sends.
  • Every photo is reviewed before it goes on screen, so keep it party-appropriate.
