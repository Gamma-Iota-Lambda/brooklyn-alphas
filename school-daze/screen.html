Once it's playing

  • The page goes full screen and keeps this computer awake.
  • Move the mouse to see the controls. They hide again after 3 seconds.
  • Keys: Space pauses, and the show starts again by itself after 3 minutes. The arrow keys go back and forward. C turns captions on or off. F is full screen. Esc leaves full screen.
  • A newly approved photo joins within a minute or two.
  • Videos the committee has added play in between the photos. Each one plays only once all of it has reached this computer, so the first showing can take a few minutes. The controls say how many are ready. After each video the School Daze logo fills the screen for a few seconds, and then the photos carry on.
  • If the connection drops, leave this page open. The photos keep playing. Don't reload the page until the connection is back.
