Executive Officers
Robert Brissett
President
Read bio: Robert Brissett

Robert crossed right here at Gamma Iota Lambda in the spring of 2007. He studied computer science at Long Island University and earned his master’s in the same field at NYU. Today he leads AI and digital transformation work in life sciences and healthcare. To Robert, OneGIL is “a standard for brotherly commitment, community engagement, and commitment to excellence,” modeled by the chapter’s founders and the legacy of our senior brother.
